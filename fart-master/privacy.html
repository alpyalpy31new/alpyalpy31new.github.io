Fart Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Contact: alpyalpy31@gmail.com

What we collect

  • An account ID from Sign in with Apple. We do not ask Apple for your name or email.
  • The nickname, avatar and country you choose. These appear on public leaderboards.
  • Your game activity: meals, shots and scores.
  • Reports and blocks you make.

What we do with it

We use this data only to run the game: leaderboards, fair scoring and moderation. We do not sell it, show ads or track you across other apps.

Who stores it

Game data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider. Sign in is handled by Apple.

On your device

Your progress and settings are saved on your phone. If you allow notifications, the game reminds you when your meal is ready. These are created on your phone.

Deleting your data

Open your profile and tap Delete account. This removes your account, scores and profile from our servers and your phone. You can also email us.

Children

Fart Master is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
